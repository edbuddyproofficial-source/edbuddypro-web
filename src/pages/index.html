Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know before you start. Still stuck? Reach out and the team will help.

Getting started

Who are these courses for?

Anyone who wants to use AI in their day-to-day work — HR, finance, product, marketing, operations, and everyday professionals. No technical background required.

Do I need any coding or technical skills?

Not at all. Every course starts from the basics and focuses on practical, no-code use of AI tools you can apply the same day.

How long does each course take?

Most tracks run 4–8 hours of content. They're broken into short lessons, so you can make progress in fifteen-minute sittings.

Are the courses self-paced?

Yes. There are no deadlines — start, pause, and resume whenever it suits you. Lifetime access to your enrolled courses is included.

What you get

Do I get a certificate?

Yes. You earn a shareable certificate on completion that you can add to your LinkedIn profile or resume.

Which AI tools do you cover?

ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Copilot and a range of role-specific tools — taught through real workflows rather than abstract theory.

Will the content stay up to date?

We update courses as the tools change, so what you learn reflects the current versions — not last year's screenshots.

Practicalities

How much do the courses cost?

Pricing varies by track. You can browse the full catalogue and preview several lessons free before deciding to enrol.

Can my company enrol a team?

Yes. We offer team plans with shared dashboards and progress tracking. Get in touch through the For Business page to set one up.

What if a course isn't right for me?

If a course isn't the right fit, reach out to support within 7 days of purchase for a full refund — no questions asked.
