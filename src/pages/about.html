About edBuddy Pro

AI skills built around the work you already do

We help working professionals use AI confidently in their actual jobs: role by role, task by task, with clear lines on what AI should and shouldn't do.

Why we started

AI tools arrived in every office almost overnight. Training didn't keep up.

Most professionals we meet have tried ChatGPT or a similar tool. Many found the answers generic, weren't sure what they were allowed to paste in, and went back to doing things the old way. The courses available were either technical, built for engineers, or general demos that didn't connect to anyone's actual week.

We built edBuddy Pro to close that gap. Each course is designed for one role, uses the tasks that role really does, and ends every module with a lab you complete on realistic work. You don't need to code, and you don't need to buy new software.

What we believe

Four principles shape every course we build.

Practical over theory

Every lesson maps to a real task, and every lab ends with something you built and can use.

Built for your role

No one-size-fits-all syllabus. An HR professional and a finance controller need different examples, different labs and different guardrails.

Honest about limits

We teach where AI is strong, where it's unreliable, and which decisions must stay with a person. Data privacy and bias aren't an appendix; they run through every course.

Help when you're stuck

Self-paced doesn't mean on your own. When a lab or a tool won't behave, you can reach a person.
