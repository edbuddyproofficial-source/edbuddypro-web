How we protect your account and your data, and how to tell us if you find a hole in it.

Your account

Passwords are stored hashed, never in plain text. Card details go straight to our payment processor and never reach our servers.

Open: hashing algorithm, session handling, whether two-factor authentication is offered, and the password policy — once the platform is built.

Our infrastructure

Traffic is served over HTTPS. Access to production is limited to the people who need it, and it's logged.

Open: hosting provider and region, encryption at rest, backup schedule and restore testing, and any certifications held.

If something goes wrong

If a breach affects your personal data we will notify you and the Data Protection Board of India, as the DPDP Act requires.

Open: the incident response process, who owns it, and the notification timeline.

Reporting a vulnerability

Found a security problem? Email hello@edbuddypro.com with enough detail to reproduce it. Give us reasonable time to fix it before going public, and don't access or change anyone else's data while testing. We won't pursue researchers who follow that.

What a good report contains

The URL or screen, what you did, what happened, and what you expected instead. A screenshot or a short recording helps. If it involves data that isn't yours, stop at the point you can prove the issue — don't go further to see how much you can reach.
